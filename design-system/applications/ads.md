# Ads

## The 3-second hallway test

A scrolling viewer gives an ad about as much attention as someone glancing down a hallway. If the headline, the offer, and a real human face aren't all legible in that glance, the ad has failed before it's read. Every static or video ad gets checked against this before it ships: cover the screen for 3 seconds, glance, look away — what landed?

## Static sizes per platform

- Instagram/Facebook feed: 1:1 (1080×1080) and 4:5 (1080×1350).
- Instagram/Facebook Stories: 9:16 (1080×1920).
- LinkedIn feed: 1:1 (1200×1200).

## Four-element composition

1. **Real photo** — a real, in-context shot (treatment room, a real student or founder) filling most of the frame. Never stock, never AI-generated.
2. **Headline** — short, serif, sentence case, stating the concrete outcome or the reader's situation (same voice rules as everywhere else — see `foundations/voice.md`).
3. **One number or proof point** — a timeframe, a result, or a named detail ("binnen circa 3 maanden"), set apart visually (small `accent/600` bubble or `brand/600` numeral, matching the Instagram photo-caption-bubble pattern).
4. **CTA** — one pill button, `accent/600` fill, `ink/900` text, 3–4 words max ("Bekijk de opleiding").

## Backgrounds

Real photography only, full-bleed. `paper` is acceptable only for a text-only or number-card-style ad variant (matching the Instagram "Quote card" / "Number card" post types) — never a gradient, never a stock background pattern.

## What is never in an ad

Stock beauty-industry photography, AI-generated people, before/after body-shaming framing, fake countdown timers or fake scarcity, hype vocabulary ("GAME CHANGER," "ontketen"), competitor call-outs, claims without a number or timeframe attached.

## Three example scripts

1. **Recognition-led**: Photo of a real student mid-treatment. Headline: "Je blijft nadenken: is dit het wel?" Proof point: "Binnen circa 3 maanden je eigen salon." CTA: "Bekijk de opleiding."
2. **No-experience-needed**: Photo of founder demonstrating technique. Headline: "Je kunt starten zonder ervaring." Proof point: "Vanaf de basis, stap voor stap." CTA: "Lees hoe het werkt."
3. **Case-study-led**: Real photo of a graduate in her own salon. Headline: "Voor de vrouw die meer wil." Proof point: "Studente, Rotterdam — eigen salon binnen 3 maanden." CTA: "Meld je aan."

## Short-form video spec

9:16, 15–30 seconds. First frame must already pass the hallway test on its own (no slow build-up, no title card with stock motion graphics — see `applications/social-instagram.md`'s Reels rule, which applies identically here). Captions burned in, sentence case, same voice as written copy. Real footage only; minimal motion graphics — at most a single `accent/600` lower-third for the CTA in the final 2–3 seconds.

## Paid copy voice

Same second-person, sentence-case, number-anchored voice as everywhere else in the brand — paid copy is not a license to write differently than organic. The only adjustment: ad copy can be shorter and more direct since attention is scarcer, but it never adopts urgency tactics or hype it wouldn't use organically.

## Success metric

Cost per qualified lead (someone who opens the actual enrollment conversation), not raw click-through rate or impressions — an ad that gets clicks from the wrong audience is a worse outcome than a quieter ad that reaches the right one.
